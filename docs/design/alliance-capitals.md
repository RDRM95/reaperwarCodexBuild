# Alliance capital silhouettes — v0.12

Original procedural fan models, not extracted game assets. Lengths retain the catalogue: Kilimanjaro 888 m (inferred), cruiser 520 m (estimate), Omaha 1,800 m (AU estimate). The hulls point +Z; exhausts sit aft and muzzles forward.

The dreadnought has a long ventral accelerator channel, pale armour rails, a forward stepped bridge, slab broadside sponsons and a squared four-engine stern. The cruiser repeats this industrial language with two engines and two battery positions per side; it has no aircraft-like capital wings. Omaha has purpose-built reinforced gun housing, recoil braces, six engines and six broadside positions per side. It is not a scaled copy of the ordinary dreadnought.

All decorative geometry merges into five material meshes per class. Model checks cover finite geometry, proportions, muzzle/exhaust placement and independent geometry between hull builds. The named hull statistics and weapon rules are unchanged.
