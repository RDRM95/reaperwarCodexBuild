### Root causes

#### RC1 — Fleet holds at one focus distance while wide formations leave 25-60% of heavy guns out of reach (flank/rear silence) (confidence: high)

- **Symptom:** Quiet standoffs: fleets hanging outside effective range, large fractions of heavy ships never fire
- **Where:** src/simulation/ai.ts:216-242 (desiredRange, approach/hold, resumeRange), ai.ts:265-281 (anchor step), ai.ts:283-296 (basis frozen in hold), ai.ts:482-522 (advance along fleet.heading only, leash, ±0.15·range trim), src/simulation/formations.ts:150-300 (battlegroup widths)
- **Mechanism:** fleetDecision stops the whole anchor when the single focus ship is within desiredRange (16.8 km for an Alliance capital core); every slot is then fixed in a basis that no longer rotates (canTurn false in hold). A ship's only range correction is a role-shared advance along the frozen fleet heading plus a trim of at most 0.15×range toward its own target, so hulls with lateral slot offsets beyond ~sqrt(range²−hold²) (≈10.9 km for a 20 km gun, ≈5 km for a 14.4 km cruiser gun) and rear ranks never get a solution. When the enemy drifts to a flank the advance axis (dot with fleet.heading) goes to zero and stations cannot follow.
- **Evidence:** EXECUTED anchor-probe layout: custom big (67 v 38) Alliance slot width 39.5 km, depth 34.1 km; 42/67 hulls cannot reach a dead-ahead focus at the 16.8 km hold, 34 even after trim; Battle of Earth canon seed 42: 25/46 (18 after trim), width 35.1 km. EXECUTED probe big harmless 150 s (no attrition): 9/36 Alliance heavy guns never in range and never fired (head seeds 1,2: 9 and 8; pre-17: 9 and 6); anchor-probe big canon: Alliance reachAny 14/34…23/32 (~60%) and Reaper 50-90% during t=35-70; Alliance bearing-to-focus vs frozen heading grows to 70-100° (canon) and 157-179° (hardcore) while hold persists. READ: ai.ts:285 canTurn excludes hold; ai.ts:508 advance uses axialDistance along fleet.heading.
- **PR #17 (be9a99c):** Not touched. Longer DN/Reaper ranges add a little reach (20 vs 16.8 km) but the hold geometry is identical; harmless-big never-in-range counts are the same (9/36 vs 9/36 seed 1).
- **Fix sketch:** Make the hold distance per role line to the nearest reachable enemy group (or nearest enemy capital cluster) instead of the focus ship; compute advance along the bearing from the role reference to that cluster, not fleet.heading; allow slow basis rotation in hold when |bearing offset| > ~30°; raise the trim for flank hulls to what their own range needs (bounded by a formation leash). Test: tests/combat-ai style harmless 67 v 38 battle for 150 s asserting every antiCapital spinal mount obtains a weaponTarget solution within 60 s of first contact and ≥90% of heavy guns have an enemy in range averaged after first contact.

#### RC2 — Target scoring prefers an out-of-range fleet focus (1.0-1.2× range) over in-range enemies, silencing narrow spinal guns (confidence: high)

- **Symptom:** Pursuit without firing; weapons failing to acquire valid targets; quiet cruisers
- **Where:** src/simulation/ai.ts:121-132 (relativeRange step ×1.15 vs 1/r², focus bonus ×(1+2·focusFire) gated relativeRange ≤ 1.2), ai.ts:148-164 (5 s lock, 1.25 margin, reachableReplacement only if current > 1.4×range), src/simulation/fireControl.ts:300-332 (weaponTarget only fires within arc), src/simulation/battle.ts:567-568 (heading faces ship.targetId)
- **Mechanism:** A fleet-focus Reaper capital at 1.0-1.2× the shooter's range scores 1/r² × 2.4 (Alliance) ≈ 2× an in-range destroyer; chooseTarget commits to it, updateMovement points the hull at it, and a 12-15° spinal arc cannot bear on the in-range enemy, so weaponTarget's target-of-opportunity fallback finds nothing. The lock bypass requires the current target to be beyond 1.4× range, so the state persists for the whole 5 s lock and is re-chosen.
- **Evidence:** EXECUTED target-probe (1 DN, focus RC at 1.1×20 km dead ahead, RD at 0.8× and 35° off): scores 3.044 vs 1.383, chooseTarget picks the capital, main gun fires 0 shots in 12 s with no solution; at 1.3× it picks the destroyer and fires after a 6 s slew; at 5° off the gun fires at the destroyer as opportunity target. EXECUTED big canon: DN tgtOutOfRange 10.4% of in-range time, cruisers 9.7%; big harmless head: cruisers tgtOutOfRange 18.6%/27.3%, no-solution 20%/17.3%, quiet 21%/24% (seeds 1,2) vs pre-17 2.3%/13.4%, 1.5%/13.4%, 5.4%/14.5%.
- **PR #17 (be9a99c):** Mechanism untouched (only the fighter ×3.2 line was added), but extending DN and Reaper ranges while leaving cruiser range at 14.4 km pushed the capital-line hold from 14.1 to 16.8 km, so cruisers' focus targets now sit in the 1.0-1.2× window far more often: EXECUTED two-seed worsening of cruiser no-solution time 1.5→20% and 13.4→17.3%.
- **Fix sketch:** Gate the focus bonus and the in-range bonus on this ship's reachability (focus bonus only when relativeRange ≤ 1, or score by solution feasibility: in range and within arc-or-slewable within one cooldown); let reachableReplacement trigger whenever the current target is beyond range and a candidate is inside (drop the 1.4 factor); optionally keep facing on the in-range target while the fleet focus is unreachable. Test: the target-probe geometry as a unit test asserting chooseTarget returns the in-range destroyer and a weapon_fired event for alliance_dn_main occurs within one cooldown.

#### RC3 — Capital hulls translate heading-independently with full-speed reverse and lateral thrust (confidence: high)

- **Symptom:** Capital ships manoeuvring unrealistically / like fighters; organics slide backwards at full speed while facing the enemy
- **Where:** src/simulation/motion.ts:45-47,65 (axial force may be negative at full acceleration), motion.ts:59-62 (lateral fraction forced to 1 within max(8 km, 3·length) or under any avoidance), motion.ts:66-77 (fraction scales acceleration only; speed capped only by maxSpeed), src/simulation/battle.ts:567-570 (heading always faces the target), src/simulation/ai.ts:491,509,520-521 (organic reposition up to 0.25·range astern)
- **Mechanism:** The arrival controller is a 3D point mover; the hull's heading is slaved to the target and translation ignores it except for a lateral acceleration scalar that is bypassed within 8 km of the goal and never caps speed. The organic reposition branch places the station up to 5 km astern, so dreadnoughts back away at 100% max speed while pointing their bow at the enemy; stations beside the ship are reached by full-speed sideways slides.
- **Evidence:** EXECUTED motion-probe (advanceMotion direct): DN goal 5 km astern → peak speed 0.260 km/s = 100% of max in pure reverse, arrives in 25 s; goal 12 km abeam → peak lateral 100% of max; Reaper capital identical (100%/100%). - **PR #17 (be9a99c):** Lowered Reaper lateral fractions only (reaper_destroyer 0.62→0.36, reaper_capital 0.28→0.12); ineffective because of the 8 km override and the absence of a speed cap (EXECUTED: Reaper capital still peaks at 100% lateral speed); organics untouched.
- **Fix sketch:** Cap reverse axial speed and lateral speed per role (e.g. capitals ≤ 0.25×maxSpeed astern, ≤ 0.35×maxSpeed abeam), shrink the omnidirectional zone to ~1.5 hull lengths, and give capitals a turn-then-burn velocity-direction limiter (velocity heading slews at the class yaw rate) while gun bearing is handled by arcs; make organic reposition a slow 'open the range' at ≤ 0.3×maxSpeed. Test: tests/motion.test.ts case — DN with goal 5 km astern never exceeds 0.25×maxSpeed reverse and rotates its heading toward the travel direction within N s.

#### RC4 — Reaper fleets never reach 'hold' in multi-ship battles, so the formation basis keeps rotating toward each new focus (confidence: high)

- **Symptom:** Advance/retreat and lateral oscillation of anchors and stations; Reaper lines swinging; station churn
- **Where:** src/simulation/ai.ts:193-213 (focus refresh, 8 s lock, 1.25 margin, hardcore factors), ai.ts:229-242 (dist measured anchor→focus ship), ai.ts:285-296 (canTurn while approach, rotation at min(0.12, turnRate/2, slowest/2/radius))
- **Mechanism:** The anchor's hold test uses the distance to the current focus ship; with 13-19 focus changes per 150 s and organics repositioning astern, the Reaper anchor rarely gets within desiredRange, stays in 'approach' for the whole battle, and canTurn therefore stays true, so every focus change rotates the entire slot basis; lateral slots of 15-20 km swing kilometres per change and every hull chases its moving station.
- **Evidence:** EXECUTED probe big canon seed 1: Reaper fleet approachSec 150/150, focusChanges 15, heading swing 72°, anchor path 38.7 km for ~32 km net; Battle of Earth head s42: reaper-sol approach 137/180 s, 19 focus changes, heading swing 166.5° with 5 yaw-rate reversals (pre-17: 330°, 9 reversals). - **Fix sketch:** Measure the anchor's hold distance to the enemy formation centre or nearest enemy capital cluster rather than the focus ship; separate 'fleet objective' (movement) from 'fleet focus' (fire); rate-limit basis rotation to one adjustment per ~20 s and ≤15° unless the bearing offset exceeds 45°; replace the Reaper brake snap (position = ship.pos) with a projection of the station onto the forward half-space. Test: big canon 150 s asserting the Reaper fleet enters hold within 60 s of first contact and total heading swing < 40°.

#### RC5 — Bounded organic reposition pins dreadnoughts 5 km astern and the anchor catch-up throttle then freezes the fleet at the enemy's preferred range (confidence: high)

- **Symptom:** Retreat/advance oscillation ending in a static exchange at the Reaper's preferred range; organics unable to re-open or close range

### Root causes

#### FS1 — Constant-pitch group lattice makes every large organic fleet the same scaled rectangle/crescent (confidence: high)

- **Symptom:** Mixed Alliance fleets look repetitive zoomed out: uniform spacing, even density, same silhouette per size; PR #20's 'salients/reserves' are invisible
- **Where:** src/simulation/formations.ts:181-184 (clearance, lateralStride, rearStride, wings), :187-197 (wave/column lattice), :198-215 (bow/wedge terms), :223-232 (flank/advance/commitment jitter), :254 (0.72 compression)
- **Mechanism:** Group centres are placed on wings=ceil(sqrt(1.6·G)) columns × waves rows with ONE lateral pitch (widest template + clearance) and ONE wave pitch (deepest template + clearance, 11.57 km for every Alliance mix because defensive_screen's 17-unit arc is always among the templates). The seeded jitter is bounded to ±0.37 pitch laterally, ±0.44 pitch in depth and a ±0.28-pitch (3.2 km) 'reserve/salient' shift for 35% of groups, so no group ever leaves its cell: no gaps, no concentrations, no change of frontage or depth. Bow terms add a smooth parabola. The 0.72 lateral compression squares the block. Result: aspect ≈1.6-1.8:1 at 200, 1000 and 3000 hulls; the silhouette is a filled, bowed slab whose size grows with N.
- **Evidence:** EXECUTED (layout.mts): A200 5×3 lattice 72×3.2×40 km; A1000 11×7 158×3.5×97 km; A3000 19×12 325×3.7×201 km; wave pitch 11.57 km in all cases, lateral pitch 12.6-16.9 km; density-grid seed correlation 0.60-0.67 at 3000 hulls; nn-distance CV 0.30-0.41. READ: jitter constants at :224-231.
- **PR #17 (be9a99c):** Not touched: formations.ts is byte-identical before and after be9a99c (EXECUTED diff); t=0 deployment metrics are identical in both checkouts.
- **Fix sketch:** Replace the uniform lattice with a seeded order-of-battle composer inside assignBattleGroups: (1) assign groups to echelons drawn from seeded weights (main body, 1-2 flank/vanguard concentrations, picket screen, reserve, optional detached salient); (2) place main-body groups along the front with per-gap pitch gap_i = base·(0.55+1.1·noise) and seeded skipped cells (1 in 4-6) so corridors and concentrations exist; (3) cap line formations at 2 ranks and grow width instead, put the reserve 1.5-2 pitches back and laterally offset, the screen 0.5-1 pitch forward in a thin arc; (4) give each echelon a seeded yaw (±15-25°) and lateral/vertical offset so the outline is a bent, uneven line rather than a rectangle.
